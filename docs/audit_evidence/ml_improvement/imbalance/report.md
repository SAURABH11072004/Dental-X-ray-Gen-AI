# Class-imbalance experiment: finding classifier (validation split only)

Date 2026-10-15. Machine-readable results: `results.json`; per-run configurations and input hashes: `configs.json`; per-seed runs:
`../cls/<exp>_s<seed>.json` with best-epoch validation probabilities `../cls/<exp>_s<seed>_valprob.npz`; frozen decision rule:
`frozen_thresholds.json`. Generated by `scripts/ml_imbalance_report.py`. **No calibration or test data was read.**

## Verdict

**No method beat the existing baseline (C0, inverse-frequency class weights): not A-D, and not Variant E.** By the decision rules
fixed before the runs finished (paired macro-F1 gain in at least 4 of 5 seeds *and* larger than the baseline seed SD of 0.027), none
qualifies. For the priority metrics, every alternative **lowered** Deep Caries recall compared with C0. A-D also lowered Periapical
Lesion recall, trading minority recall for minority precision. E raised Periapical Lesion recall by +0.044 on average (less than one
crop out of 9) while cutting Deep Caries recall in every seed. Validation-tuned decision thresholds did not hold up under
cross-fitting. Nothing is promoted; the production classifier is unchanged, and the imbalance problem is not solved.

## Protocol

* ResNet-50 (ImageNet), 224 px ground-truth tooth crops, 15 epochs, batch 32, AdamW lr 3e-4 wd 1e-4, cosine schedule; seeds 42-46.
* Fit on train (1550 / 438 / 412 / 120 crops), best epoch by validation macro-F1, metrics on the same validation split (331 crops;
  supports 205 / 45 / 72 / 9). Using validation for both epoch choice and reporting biases every method upward by the same mechanism;
  the comparison is fair, the absolute numbers are optimistic.
* No early stopping: every run trains 15 epochs and keeps its best validation epoch.
* Inputs identical for every new run (SHA-256): train crops `293cbc38…`, validation crops `fc58b21c…`, split assignments
  `1c350480eed2ae96`, manifest `932b0b94…` (the same manifest the deployed checkpoints were trained on).
* Exactly one factor differs from C0 per method. SD is the sample SD (ddof=1); the earlier `cls_summary.md` used ddof=0, which is
  why it shows 0.024 for the value reported here as 0.027.

## Phase 1: reproducibility

The C0 re-run (C0r, current code) reproduced the original C0 runs exactly on all 5 seeds: identical macro-F1, best epoch and
confusion matrix (`results.json` -> `reproducibility`). Training is deterministic on this machine for this protocol, so differences
between methods are not run-to-run noise of the same seed. They are still subject to seed variation (SD 0.027 in macro-F1).

Earlier runs `C_sampler`, `C4_weights_sqrt` and `C3_oversample` used `aug=conservative`, so each changed two factors at once versus C0.
They are superseded here by single-factor runs with baseline augmentation. `C3b_oversample_baselineaug` (single factor, run
earlier with the same code path) is reused for B. `C3c_oversample_noflip` stopped at 4 seeds.

Reading:
* **A and B** keep macro-F1 roughly level by shifting the operating point toward precision: higher accuracy and minority precision,
  clearly lower minority recall. If missed lesions matter more than false alarms, that is the wrong direction.
* **C (focal)** is worse than C0 on macro-F1 in all 5 seeds.
* **D (sqrt weights)** is worse on macro-F1 and fails the side-effect rule (Periapical Lesion F1 −0.078 on average).
* **E (geometric augmentation on Deep Caries and Periapical Lesion crops only)** fails both rules: macro-F1 −0.013 (better in 1 of 5
  seeds), and Deep Caries F1 −0.062 (worse than the 0.05 limit) because Deep Caries recall fell in all 5 seeds (0.587 -> 0.462).
  Periapical Lesion recall rose by +0.044 on average (0.711 -> 0.756), which is +0.4 of a crop out of 9; it improved in 2 seeds and
  was unchanged in 3, with PL precision −0.025. This is not evidence of a real gain for that class.
* **Periapical Lesion has 9 validation crops**, so its recall moves in steps of 0.111 and differences of one crop dominate. The
  validation split cannot resolve small real differences for this class.

## Phase 4: class-specific decision thresholds (C0r probabilities)

Multiplicative class weights on the softmax (Caries fixed at 1) were fitted on validation by coordinate ascent on macro-F1, and judged
by 2-fold cross-fitting grouped by radiograph, repeated 20 times per seed.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 0.747 | 0.781 | 0.763 | 1, 2.24, 1, 1 |
| 43 | 0.730 | 0.751 | 0.732 | 1, 3.56, 0.45, 1 |
| 44 | 0.799 | 0.809 | 0.769 | 1, 0.35, 0.79, 1 |
| 45 | 0.776 | 0.803 | 0.769 | 1, 0.79, 1, 0.56 |
| 46 | 0.768 | 0.791 | 0.763 | 1, 1.41, 1, 0.50 |
| mean | 0.764 | 0.787 (+0.023) | 0.759 (−0.005) | unstable across seeds |

The in-sample gain disappears under cross-fitting, and the fitted weights disagree between seeds (Deep Caries 0.35 to 3.56). Tuned
thresholds are therefore **not adopted**; the frozen rule is plain argmax (`frozen_thresholds.json`). Thresholds change only the
operating point; they are not an improvement of the learned representation.

validation-based choice; it is documented, not adopted.

## Runtime and failures

* New runs: 20 (A, C, D and C0r, 5 seeds each), 10:38:48-11:52:59 on 2026-10-15 (74 min). 214-239 s per run, peak GPU 1825-1832 MB.
* **Interruption:** after I_focal finished (11:52:59), the batch was stopped by Claude Code because system memory was critically low,
  before E started. No partial results existed and nothing was lost.
* **E was run separately** on request, 12:11-12:31 (5 seeds, `--only-new`, so no completed run was repeated). Pre-flight: no other
  training process, GPU 46 °C idle with no thermal throttling, 4.2 GB RAM free, on AC power. 226-249 s per run, peak GPU 1828-1830 MB,
  exit 0. Its augmentation was checked on real crops first (`../visual/minority_geom_examples.png`: geometry applied to Deep Caries
  and Periapical Lesion only, 0 times to Caries/Impacted); validation crops are never augmented.
* Candidates: 25 best-epoch state dicts in `runtime/candidates/`. `Data/checkpoints` was not touched; the production classifier's
  SHA-256 is unchanged (`5aa878e1fc9ef097`).

## What this does and does not show

* It shows that, on this validation split, the existing inverse-frequency weighting is at least as good as sampling, oversampling,
  focal loss, sqrt weighting and minority-only geometric augmentation for macro-F1, and better for Deep Caries recall.
* **The class-imbalance problem is not solved.** Deep Caries (F1 0.58) and Periapical Lesion (F1 0.66, 9 validation crops) remain the
  weak classes under every method tested. The production classifier stays as it is.
* It does not show what happens on independent data: validation also chose the epoch, and the minority classes are tiny (45 and 9 crops).
* No combined method was run, because no single method justified it.
* More labelled Deep Caries and Periapical Lesion examples remain the measure most likely to help; none of the training-side
  changes tested here added information.
